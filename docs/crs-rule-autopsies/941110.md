## What stood out to me

`REQUEST_HEADERS` is a target, but `!REQUEST_HEADERS:Cookie` cuts one header back out of it. Same syntax I use when tuning.

The regex is dead simple. The transformations do the heavy lifting: six decoders run before it, and that's what makes it hard to sneak past.

Several XSS rules pile onto the same payload. Score 20, way over the threshold. That's why bumping the threshold to 10 doesn't let this one through (see my [anomaly scoring experiment](../anomaly-scoring-experiment.md)).

The entity encoded version tripped 932235 as well. After `&lt;` decodes, `;script` looks like a shell command. Hence 25.

## What triggers false positives

People writing *about* HTML. "My kid typed `<script>` into the search box" came up while I was hunting for FP-001. Blocked, score 15.

## Where I'd tune it

A route + argument exclusion for fields that are supposed to hold markup. Otherwise I leave it alone. The real fix for XSS is output encoding in the app anyway.
